INTRODUCTION
1.1  General and safety-related  information
The rolling cassette type CB, which is described in this manual, has been developed for the cold rolling of steel rods with a maximum entry diameter of 14 mm.
It is composed of two opposed Sets of 3 rolls; the rolls of each triad are at a 120° angle to each other.
The simultaneous opening/closing of the rolls of each Set is carried out manually by means of the suitable crank supplied with the machine.
The rolling cassettes are intended to be added to a transformation line equipped, in particular, with a pulling block which provides the force necessary to pull the strand through the rolls.
The operating instructions contained in this manual are part of the technical documentation of the supply and are an integral part of the documentation of the line where the rolling cassette is inserted.
The information contained herein is intended for the personnel responsible for the installation, use and maintenance of the cassette .
In order to keep operators free from risk and danger and avoid damage to the equipment parts, it is essential to be familiar with this Manual before carrying out any operation on the equipment or its parts.
Full  knowledge  of  and  compliance  with  the  instructions  provided  in  this  manual are  essential conditions  for the  proper   handling  and  maintenance  of  the  cassette. The  manual  must  be kept near the  rolling cassettes,  in a safe  place, where  it can  be available to all the people  required to consult it.   Furthermore, it must be unabridged and entirely readable.
Both the operational and maintenance personnel must have a copy of this manual.
Careful compliance with the instructions contained in this manual, during the installation, handling and maintenance phases, is the essential condition for proper and long-lasting operation of the cassette.
EUROLLS S.p.A. is not to be held responsible for any damage caused to personnel or machine resulting from non-compliance with the instructions contained herein.
The rolling cassettes must only be used by personnel who has read, understood and learnt the instructions contained in this Manual and in any case, only by trained and qualified personnel.
These Manual's Instructions neither can in any way replace, supplement or modify any Standard, Prescription, Decree, Directive or Law on the specific matter in force In the place of Installation and concerning the safety, use and maintenance nor can be a substitute for the personnel training.
Should any part of this manual appear insufficiently clear to the reader, EUROLLS S.p.A. technical department is available to provide all necessary information and clarifications.

GENERAL DESCRIPTION OF THE CASSETTE
The cassette described in this manual is suitable for the rolling of smooth or ribbed low-carbon steel wire within the following diameters: max entry diameter 14 mm, minimum exit wire diameter 3,0 mm.
The cassette does not operate independently but is usually mounted onto a rolling line equipped with a pulling head.
The steel wire, anchored to the pulling head, goes through the idle rolling rolls, its section undergoes a cold plastic deformation due to the necking down by means of the profiles on the two Sets; the section is thus reduced and an exit diameter smaller than the entry diameter is obtained.
The rolls are mounted on suitable roll holders equipped with bearings and lie at a 120° angle to each other (Set of 3 Rolls - system).
The roll holders are mounted onto the cassette by means of pins with a series of holes and linked to a cooling system in order to disperse the heat generated during the rolling process.
To this end the cassette is equipped with points where it is possible to add cooling fluid as well as with points to which the discharge pipes for the warm fluid can be attached, so that it can reach the cooling system; this ensures the constant availability of cooling fluid at a suitable temperature to enter the cassette.
Each cassette is equipped with two independent cooling systems, one on each of the two different wire entry and exit groups.
These two groups are equipped with the following types of rolls, depending on what kind of rolled material shall be produced:
STRUCTURAL RIBBED WIRE
The first set at the wire ENTRY "Forming rolls FO" reduces the wire section to a triangular shape so that the wire is ready to enter the following set, where the ribbing takes place.
The function of the second triad at the wire EXIT "Ribbing rolls PR" is to obtain a ribbed wire according to the customer's specific requirements.

Cooling system
The cassette is equipped with a cooling system to dissipate the heat generated by the bearings inside the roll holders during the rolling process.
To this end the pins supplied together with the cassette are equipped with holes through which the cooling fluid flows in order to ensure that the bearings of the roll holders always operate within the ideal temperature range for correct operation.
The specifications of the cooling fluid, usually water, shall be as follows:
Only filtered and decalcified water shall be used	Ph 6-6.5

2.5  Lifting and handling the rolling cassette
The points shown in the Fig. 6 (points "A" and "B"), relating to the 2 eyebolts supplied with the machine, are used to lift and move the cassette.
While lifting or moving the cassette, sharp movements and dangerous oscillations shall be avoided.
WARNING
All lifting operations must be carried out exclusively by authorized and qualified personnel.
WARNING
Abrupt movements of the cassette during handling may damage the rolling cassette and have an effect on its proper operation.
In addition, damp can cause damage to the cassette so always keep it dry.
WARNING
We recommend you to verify the correlation between the pulling angle and the values given in the table supplied by the rope, chain or sling manufacturer and using lifting devices with a capacity at least 30% greater than the load to lift, which is to be inferred from the chart "Technical specifications" (see par. 2.1 Tab 1).

ADJUSTING THE ROLLING CASSETTE
After positioning and correctly aligning the cassette on the wiredrawing machine:
fix it by means of screws M12 supplied (Fig.7 ref. A)
connect it to the water circuit (Fig. 5)
verify that there is grease in the roll holders by pumping new grease from the relevant lubrication units (Fig. 4-a ref. 1)
insert the wire to be rolled into the cassette.
Insertion of the Wire into the Cassette
In order to insert the wire correctly into the cassette, proceed as follows:
open the two Sets of rolls by using the relevant crank supplied with the machine (Fig. 8, ref. A) and by turning into the direction "OPENED" (Fig.8, ref. B)
This operation is carried out by acting on the 2 reduction units to open/close the rolls (Fig. 3 ref. C and 8), which enable the simultaneous opening of the two Sets independently.

Verify that the inserted wire remains between the profiles of the three rolls of each Set; should it accidentally slip out, this could damage the tungsten carbide rolls when the force necessary to roll the inserted material is applied.
WARNING
Verify that the wire is in the center in between the rolls so as to avoid spoiling the workpiece and cause possible damage to the rolls.
Correctly anchor the wire exiting the cassette to the capstan or to the pulling block and verify that the wire is correctly aligned and orthogonal to the working axis of the cassette.
Begin the alternating closing of the two triads of rolls.
Close in advance the two triads of rolls so as to enable the rolls and the material to be rolled to come into touch. (When this happens, there is a light resistance to the closing force).
Proceed then to close alternatively the rolls and activate the capstan or pulling block, in strict observance of the specifications of the producer of the wiredrawing machine so as to ensure complete operational safety and to obtain, at the exit of the cassette, a product corresponding to the requirements.
If the size of the exiting wire does not correspond to the requirements, repeat the adjusting procedure until a product with the required features is obtained.
The size of the exiting wire shall be measured every time the rolls are adjusted.
The value observed on the graduated scale shown in Fig. 9 shall be used as reference for the position of the rolls.
In this case, the fixed graduated scale "O - 60" on the front side of the cassette will serve as verification for the reference notch on the central rolling body, so that it can be put back into the same position as before, without having to carry out the adjustments again, thus saving time.
